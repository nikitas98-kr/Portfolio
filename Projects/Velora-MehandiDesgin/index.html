Frequently Asked Questions

~How long does the mehndi take to apply?

It usually takes 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the design’s size and details.

~How long will the mehndi color last?

The stain typically lasts 7–10 days, depending on your skin type and aftercare.

~Do you use natural and safe mehndi?

Yes bro! We use 100% natural, chemical-free mehndi that is safe for all skin types.

~How can I make the mehndi color darker?

Keep the mehndi on for 4–6 hours, avoid water, and apply clove steam or sugar-lemon mixture for best results.
